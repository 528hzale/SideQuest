# Sidequest: General Description (Revised Masterfile)

Sidequest is a location discovery app that helps users find interesting places to visit, such as cafes, food stalls, scenic spots, tourist attractions, and activities. It provides personalized recommendations based on the user's preferences, location, budget, and interests, while helping local businesses attract more customers through promotions and exclusive deals.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Explore Places
Users see nearby places such as:
- Cafes
- Restaurants
- Food stalls
- Tourist spots
- Scenic locations
- Activities

### 2. Search & Filters
Users can search based on:
- Category
- Distance
- Price
- Rating
- Open/closed
- Location

### 3. Personalized Recommendations
Sidequest recommends places based on what the user wants.

Example: *"I want somewhere chill, under ₱300, within 2 km."*
Sidequest gives suitable recommendations.

### 4. "Surprise Me"
The user doesn't know where to go. They tap **Give Me a Sidequest**, and the app randomly recommends a suitable place. This is one of Sidequest's signature features.

### 5. Place Details
Each location shows:
- Photos
- Description
- Address
- Map location
- Opening hours
- Price range
- Ratings/reviews
- Available deals
- Transportation details
- Registration details (optional)
- Difficulty/safety level (optional)

### 6. Save / Favorites
Users can save places they want to visit later.

### 7. Deals & Discounts
Special offers from partner businesses.

Example: *20% OFF at Café X — Valid until September 15 — [Claim Deal]*
This directly supports the Deals & Discounts revenue model.

### 8. Check-In
Users check in when they visit a place, supporting the Pay Per Visit model.
Flow: **User → Scan Business QR → Check-in recorded**

### 9. Reviews & Ratings
Users can rate places and leave short reviews to help others decide where to go.

### 10. Event Scheduler
Users can see upcoming events and view which food stalls, photobooth stalls, and other businesses will be involved.

### 11. Photo Bank (New)
Users can upload photos of the places they visit, either during check-in or anytime from a place's page.
- **Place Page Gallery** — uploaded photos appear in a scrollable gallery on the place's detail page, alongside official photos. Sortable by Most Recent or Most Liked. Each photo credits the uploader.
- **Newsfeed** — uploading a photo can optionally post it to a lightweight, Instagram-style newsfeed, showing the user's avatar, the place name, the photo, a caption, and a "Visited via Sidequest" tag.
- **Moderation basics** — a simple report/flag button per photo, with a choice between pre-publish review or auto-publish with post-hoc moderation.

## Gamification (Kept Light by Design)

Gamification is intentionally kept simple so it stays fun for casual users too — not just users who visit often or compete hard. Mechanics that could make points feel out of reach for casual users (like public leaderboards or reviewer-ranking competitions) were left out.

### Quest Points (QP)
A single point currency earned through everyday actions:
- Checking in at a place
- Writing a review
- Using "Give Me a Sidequest"
- Uploading a photo
- Claiming a deal
- Saving a place to Favorites

### Explorer Rank
A light, game-flavored progression system based on QP, inspired by the "Adventurer Rank" concept in reincarnation/fantasy anime — familiar and easy to understand even without genre knowledge:

Bronze Explorer → Silver Explorer → Gold Explorer → Platinum Explorer → Mythril Explorer

Higher ranks unlock small, tangible perks (bigger deal discounts, early access to new places/events) rather than bragging rights alone, so the reward feels earned rather than competitive.

### Explorer Card
A simplified profile page, styled like a lightweight "character card," showing:
- Current Rank
- QP total
- Places visited
- Badges earned
- Streak count

No complicated stat systems — just real, meaningful numbers users already understand.

### Specialty Tag
An auto-assigned identity tag based on visit history — no point-spending or menus required:
Foodie Explorer, Culture Seeker, Scenic Wanderer, Nightlife Explorer, Budget Hunter

Shown on the Explorer Card and next to reviews, adding a bit of personality and credibility (e.g., a "Foodie Explorer" review of a food stall carries more weight).

### Badges
Small, achievable goals that reward variety, not just volume:
- Coffee Connoisseur — visit 10 cafes
- First Sidequest — complete your first "Surprise Me" visit
- Reviewer — write 10 reviews
- Trailblazer — among the first 20 check-ins at a new place

### Streaks
- Daily streak for opening the app or checking in
- Weekly Sidequest streak for visiting at least one new place a week
- Optional streak freeze (earned with QP) so one missed day doesn't erase progress

### Digital Stamp Book / City Passport
Each visited place earns a stamp on a digital passport, grouped into themed collections (e.g., "Naga Food Trail," "Bicol Scenic Spots"). Completing a collection unlocks a small reward. Gives users a visual sense of progress across the city.

### Surprise Me Mystery Box
Completing a "Give Me a Sidequest" visit opens a small mystery reward — bonus QP, a discount, or a rare badge — turning the app's signature feature into its strongest gamification touchpoint.

### Reward Redemption
QP can be spent, not just collected:
- Redeem QP for bigger discounts on partner deals
- Redeem QP for exclusive Sidequest-only perks
- Occasional QP-funded raffle sponsored by a partner business

This ties gamification directly back into the Deals & Discounts and Pay Per Visit revenue models.

## Revenue Model

### 1. Sponsored / Promoted Places
Businesses pay Sidequest to appear more prominently in recommendations and search results, helping them attract more potential customers.

### 2. Pay Per Visit / Customer Referral
Sidequest earns a small fee whenever a user visits a business through the app, verified via check-in or QR code.

### 3. Deals and Discounts
Businesses offer exclusive discounts or promotions through Sidequest. Sidequest earns by charging businesses for promoting these deals, while users get cheaper offers.
